01 / WHY WE EXISTBUILT FOR COMMERCE STUDENTS

LEARN WITH PURPOSE

Commerce students deserve a place to grow.

Commerce League is a student-focused learning platform built to bring commerce learning, practical work, and healthy competition into one place.

From understanding accounting concepts to finding useful learning material, our goal is to make it easier to practise, explore, and keep improving—without constantly searching across different websites.

MADE FOR STUDENTS

Learning should not come with a price tag.

Commerce League is currently free to use. It was created as a student service, with the intention of keeping learning accessible rather than charging students to participate.

No subscription is required to explore the open learning resources.

02 / THE LEAGUEHEALTHY COMPETITION · SHARED GROWTH

FIND YOUR PEOPLE

A little competition can help you go further.

In school, a healthy challenge from someone at a similar level can give you a reason to prepare, ask better questions, and keep going. The League is designed around that spirit.

Students qualify through an entrance test and are intended to be grouped by performance, so they can learn alongside peers working at a comparable level. Shared interests and ambitions—whether accounting, business, or a future CA path—can give students more to learn from one another.

League participation and group arrangements are coordinated by the organiser. The website currently stores each student's profile locally; it does not provide built-in chat or automatic student matching.

01

Qualify

Complete the entrance process set by your organiser.

02

Find your level

Performance-based grouping is intended to make challenges fair and motivating.

03

Learn together

Exchange ideas, ask questions, and learn from peers with shared goals.

04

Keep improving

Use preparation and healthy competition to notice what to work on next.
